Details

Good to know

How far ahead should I book?

Four to six weeks for most dates, and three months or more for weddings and bank holiday weekends. If your date is sooner, still ask — a couple of slots are held each week for short notice.

Do you install, or do I collect?

We deliver, install and return for collection. Everything is built on site so nothing arrives creased or squashed. Travel within [X] miles is included; beyond that we add [£X] per mile.

How long do the balloons last?

Air-filled installations hold their shape for several days indoors, so we can build the day before. Helium floats for roughly 12–24 hours untreated, or up to three days treated. Outdoor installations are air-filled only.

Can you match an exact colour?

Yes. Send a photograph, a fabric swatch or a hex code — or build the palette yourself in the Palette Studio above. We'll match as closely as latex allows and show you the colour board before anything is ordered.

Do you work outdoors?

Yes, on weighted frames rated for wind. We'll tell you honestly if the forecast makes it a poor idea, and suggest a sheltered position on the day.

What do you need from my venue?

An access window, somewhere to park, and confirmation of what may be fixed to walls or ceilings. We're fully insured and can send the certificate directly to your venue.
