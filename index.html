How it works

The very complicated process

Four steps. Three of them are ours.

01

You message us

Date, venue, rough idea. We'll come back the same day and hold your slot for 48 hours, free.

02

We have ideas

You get colours and a sketch of it in your actual space, with a fixed price. Change it as often as you like.

03

We make lots of balloons

Built on site in your access window, usually 60–120 minutes. Nothing turns up pre-squashed in a van.

04

You take all the credit

Say you did it yourself, we won't tell. We come back for it after so nobody's dismantling an arch at midnight.
