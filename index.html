The studio

We don't sell balloons. We design the fifteen seconds everybody films.

Every installation begins with your palette — a swatch from the dress, a hex code from your brand book, a photograph of the room at four in the afternoon when the light is right. From there it's drawn to scale against your venue, so nothing is guessed on the day.

Everything is built on site by hand. Nothing arrives pre-made in the back of a van, creased into a shape it will never recover from. And when the last guest has gone, we come back for it — collection is booked the moment the install is.
